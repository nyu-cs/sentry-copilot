# Strategy selection snapshot

## Purpose

The strategy-selection screen is the primary collection point for up to four in-session strategies.
The reducer-owned current snapshot is authoritative. Evidence may eventually come from the
selection screen, a fallback strategy panel, or explicit user correction, but all accepted values
merge into the same `StrategySelectionSnapshot`.

M0.1a is complete when the unique strategies of the actual one-to-four participants who entered
battle can be stored and queried. It does not require complete player identity or runtime-slot
association.

## Participant identity

- `session_player_id` is opaque, generated by the assistant, and valid only for one session.
- `player_tag` is stored as four digits such as `"0038"` and displayed as `#0038`.
- `player_tag` is session-local evidence, not a global account primary key.
- `selection_row` identifies a row on the selection screen only.
- `selection_row` must never be copied to a runtime slot.
- `avatar_visual_key` is neither a strategy nor a unique identity.
- `selection_outcome` is `entered_battle`, `left_unready`, `exited_before_strategy`,
  `exited_after_strategy`, or `unknown`.
- Selection outcome does not reuse runtime `LEFT`, `DISCONNECTED`, or `ELIMINATED`.
- Only `entered_battle` belongs to the final team. Other outcomes remain historical selection
  records.
- Display names are presentation and supporting evidence; they do not determine identity
  completeness.

## Field-level evidence

Every known participant field has its own `EvidenceRecord`:

- `player_tag`
- `display_name`
- `avatar`
- `strategy`
- `ready`
- `is_self`
- `selection_outcome`

Each record contains an evidence source, confidence, observation time, and optional source detail.
Observation times must be timezone-aware. Naive datetimes are rejected at model boundaries.
Evidence priority is:

```text
manual > observed > derived > predicted
```

Within the same source, higher confidence wins. If confidence is equal, the newer observation wins.
Unknown values remain `None`.

Participants, snapshots, their participant/evidence collections, and field-evidence mappings are
immutable after validation. Reducer operations always build and validate replacement values. The
Python representation uses tuples and an immutable mapping; JSON still uses arrays and objects.

## Completeness

`expected_participant_count` is an explicit value from one to four, or `None` while it remains
unknown. It must not be inferred from `participants` length or the number of successful
recognitions. A frozen snapshot with an unknown expected count remains partial.

```text
PARTIAL
The snapshot is not frozen, expected participant count is unknown, entered participant count does
not match it, or at least one entered strategy is unknown.

STRATEGIES_COMPLETE
The frozen snapshot has the expected number of entered participants and each has a unique strategy,
but at least one entered player tag is unknown.

FULLY_IDENTIFIED
Strategies are complete and every entered participant has a valid unique four-digit player tag.
```

Names, avatars, ready state, self recognition, and runtime slots do not affect these levels.
Single-player `expected_participant_count=1` and a three-player match with an expected count of
three can therefore become strategy-complete. Three observed entered players with an expected count
of four remain partial. `expected_participant_count` is the final number that entered battle, not
the number initially shown or currently alive.

`left_unready`, `exited_before_strategy`, and `exited_after_strategy` do not enter the final count
or default `TeamStrategyContext`. They remain in the raw snapshot. Temporary strategies on
selection-stage exits do not participate in final-team strategy uniqueness and may duplicate an
entered participant's strategy.

## Freeze and correction

A snapshot may be frozen while partial. After freezing:

- ordinary observations may fill missing fields;
- ordinary observations cannot replace a conflicting final strategy;
- same-value observations may strengthen field evidence;
- explicit manual correction may replace frozen values;
- multi-participant corrections validate atomically;
- a correction cannot create a new participant.

Manual field evidence is accepted only through `StrategySelectionSnapshotCorrected`.
Correction merges each field into a candidate copy. Changed values require and adopt manual
evidence. Unchanged values keep their current evidence unless the replacement supplies stronger
evidence under the normal priority rules. The reducer validates the complete candidate snapshot
once, so any failed participant leaves the original session state unchanged.

## Runtime independence

The snapshot is a historical selection record. Runtime health and status are separate:

- `hp <= 0` changes only runtime `PlayerState`;
- runtime inactivation never changes `selection_outcome` or removes snapshot participants;
- runtime inactivation never clears or releases an entered participant's strategy ID;
- historical `TeamStrategyContext` continues to include strategies after runtime inactivation;
- current survivors and snapshot participant count are independent.

An entered player always retains `selection_outcome=entered_battle`, whether runtime departure
occurs during an ordinary round, between ordinary play and secret core, or in any secret-core wave.

Future runtime state separates participation (`active` or terminal `inactive`), inactivation reason
(`left_or_disconnected`, `hp_depleted`, or `unknown`), and inactive presentation (`departed`,
`spectating`, or `unknown`). Disconnect is not a separate business reason. Departed presentation
alone does not establish HP depletion; spectating is positive HP-depletion evidence but does not
mean the player still contributes. Once inactive, later HP, actions, and contribution are ignored.

Future transition records will carry `observed_at`, `stage_type`, optional round and wave,
previous/new participation status, reason, presentation, optional HP, confidence, and evidence.
Runtime stage type will distinguish at least `normal` and `secret_core`; secret core permits
`round_number=None`. Per-wave checks, continuous player-bar observation, status-change events,
wave-boundary roster checkpoints, and an inactive-filtering active-team query are deferred live
features, not part of M0.1a.

## Legacy runtime strategy fields

`PlayerState.strategy_id` remains temporarily for compatibility with the seed scaffold. It is not
updated from the new snapshot and is not read by `build_team_strategy_context`. Removal is deferred
until runtime association is implemented and reviewed separately.

## Deferred to M0.1b or later

- fallback panel observation models;
- runtime-slot association;
- matching by unique strategy or player tag;
- user-confirmed binding;
- selection-screen OCR;
- avatar recognition;
- desktop UI;
- automatic clicking;
- real strategy data and recommendation logic.
